ABOUT ORAVEL

CRAFTED AROUND
NATURE'S RHYTHM

Sustainability and regenerative agriculture are our foundational philosophy.

Every discarded apple pomace from the cold pressing stage is returned directly to the orchard soil as nutrient-dense organic compost or gifted to local wild alpine bee sanctuaries.

By working strictly in harmony with seasonal harvest cycles, ORAVEL preserves biodiversity and soil vitality across every acre of our ancestral alpine orchards.
